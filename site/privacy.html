Privacy

Last updated: November 2026.

This site has no accounts, no sign-up and no resume uploads. We don't ask for your name, email or phone number, and we don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

  • Saved jobs are stored only in your own browser (local storage). They never reach our servers. Clearing your browser data removes them.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.
  • Applying. When you click "Apply", you leave this site. The company's own privacy policy then applies to anything you share with them.
