What this site does

We collect open jobs from companies' official career pages and hiring systems and put them in one search. We don't post jobs ourselves, we don't take applications, and we never charge job seekers. Every "Apply" button takes you to the company's own website.

Where the jobs come from

  • Public job-board feeds that companies publish through Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and SmartRecruiters so their jobs can be shown on other sites.
  • Structured job data (schema.org JobPosting) on company career pages, only where the site's robots.txt allows automated reading.

We do not copy listings from other job portals. We show a short summary of each job and link to the full original posting. Listings are refreshed once a day; jobs the company has closed disappear at the next refresh. Experience and job level are estimated from the posting's text, so always check the original.

Our collector identifies itself as OpenRolesBot, waits between requests, and reads each source at most once a day.

Companies: remove or correct your listings

If you'd like your company's jobs removed or corrected, email us from your company domain. We act on removal requests within 48 hours.

Report a problem

Seen a job that looks fake, closed or wrong? Email the same address with the link, and we'll check it.
